Confirmation

Season 2 begins with 13 GYATT Laws of Fortnite, a pile of suspicious evidence, and one uncomfortable possibility: maybe the match isn’t rigged. Maybe our brains are.

About this episode

Drop In.

First...Season 2 of the GYATT Fortnite Podcast is here. And Fortnite finally did it.

They put Cheat Codes, Lobby Hacks and Overrides into the game—which sound less like new features and more like official names for everything I’ve been accusing Fortnite of doing to me for years.

After hundreds of matches, I’ve collected evidence. Not GOOD evidence. But evidence.

The weapon you need appears after you no longer need it. Waste your mobility? Somebody attacks. The bush you check is empty. The bush you DON’T check has somebody living inside it. Chase a player long enough? Somewhere during that pursuit, they’re downloading an update.

I have identified 13 GYATT Laws of Fortnite.

Then...The investigation takes a turn. Maybe Fortnite isn’t keeping track of these laws. Maybe WE are.

Every ridiculous death we remember. Every coincidence we turn into evidence. Every weapon we don’t trust. Every location we swear is cursed. Every moment that makes us yell: “SEE?! I TOLD YOU!”

There’s a name for that. Confirmation.

So was the match actually rigged? Probably not. But if I’m top three with the perfect loadout and everything starts going suspiciously well...I’m keeping my mouth shut. Season 2 has begun.
