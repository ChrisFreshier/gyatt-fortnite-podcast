In Fortnite, you can purchase the appearance of a champion, but surviving long enough to become one is a different story....

I paid for this confidence. Apparently, the Victory Royale costs extra.

Why does losing your first match with a brand-new skin feel like receiving a one-star product review? Can cosmetics actually influence how we play? And why are we spending V-Bucks to look dangerous when we still can't hit our shotgun shots? I'm speaking about you, not me lol

From intimidating skins that make us feel unstoppable to default players who humble us in seconds, we're examining the strange relationship between fashion, identity, and terrible decisions on the island....

Just remember, you look AMAZING, and apparently that's all the motivation you need to push an entire squad by yourself.
