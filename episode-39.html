About this episode

Drop In.

First.....You call it strategy. They call it the reason they turned their console off. Welcome to Episode 39 of the GYATT Fortnite Podcast, “Who’s The Villain?” where we investigate a disturbing possibility — that annoying player ruining everyone’s match might be YOU.

From chasing somebody across the island over one shot to arriving at a fight just in time to collect everybody’s loot, we’re examining the plays we celebrate when we do them and complain about when somebody returns the favor.

Then..... we’re bringing teammates into the mess, because nothing says friendship like yelling “On me!” after starting a fight nobody agreed to.

Expect questionable rescues, wounded egos, terrible excuses, and the discovery that “they came out of nowhere” sometimes means you weren’t paying attention.

Underneath the chaos is a lesson about perspective, accountability, and learning to lose. Every opponent had a story before you arrived. Every teammate deserves more than the emergency your confidence created.

So pull up, check your “halo”, and let’s take a funny look at the gap between good intentions and bad decisions. Somewhere, somebody is telling a Fortnite horror story. There’s a chance you’re the main character.
