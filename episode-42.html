About this episode

Run It Back.

First…. Fortnite might be getting a One Piece season, and I think it's beautiful that after years of searching for treasure, Luffy might finally discover a common pistol….

Then…..

One game. Endless transformations. And apparently, we’re still waiting for a sequel….

From completely redesigned maps and character appearances to gameplay overhauls, Zero Build, and the possibility of a fresh beginning, we’re exploring what a true sequel would actually need to deliver.

Could Fortnite 2 already exist in everything but name? Would a new number change anything? And if Epic reinvented the entire game tomorrow, what would we desperately want them to keep?

Rumors are circulating about what comes after Chapter 7, with whispers of “Fortnite 2,” “Book 2,” and a possible reinvention of the game we thought we knew. But how much of it is real, and how much is the internet building a mansion on somebody else’s speculation?
